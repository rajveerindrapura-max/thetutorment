Frequently asked questions

Is the first session really free?
Yes. Every first session is free, always. No card required, no conditions. If we cannot help you, we say so in the first session and do not take any payment.
How are sessions delivered?
Sessions take place over WhatsApp call, Google Meet, or Zoom. We adapt to whatever platform works best for you. Sessions typically run 30 to 45 minutes for initial consultations and up to 90 minutes for working sessions.
How quickly will someone contact me?
We aim to respond to every enquiry within 24 hours. If you need a faster response, send a message directly on WhatsApp at +91 90570 54077.
Do you work with students outside India?
Yes. The International Wing works with students and professionals across the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, Singapore, Ireland, the UAE, and beyond. All sessions are delivered remotely over video call or WhatsApp.
What if I have already tried and failed?
This is actually where we do some of our best work. If your Health and Care Professions Council application was rejected, if your Chevening essay did not make the shortlist, or if your PhD proposal was turned down, book a free session. We will go through what went wrong and tell you exactly what needs to change.
