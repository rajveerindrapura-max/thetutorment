Service 01
Health and Care Professions Council Registration for Internationally Trained Health Professionals

The Health and Care Professions Council regulates 15 health professions in the United Kingdom. If you trained outside the UK, you must apply through the international registration route before you can practice legally or use your professional title. The most common reason for rejection is a documentation error that takes minutes to fix once someone who knows the process looks at it. We review your full application, clinical hours, qualification equivalence, English language evidence, reference letters, before you submit a single page.

Service 02
Nursing and Midwifery Council Registration for Internationally Trained Nurses and Midwives

The Nursing and Midwifery Council registers all nurses, midwives and nursing associates who practice in the United Kingdom. International applicants must complete the overseas registration route including a Computer Based Test and an Objective Structured Clinical Examination. We guide you through every stage, initial application, Computer Based Test preparation, supervised practice placement support, and Objective Structured Clinical Examination preparation with a counsellor who has been through the same process.

Sponsored Job Search in the United Kingdom

To work in the United Kingdom as a non-settled person, you need a job offer from an employer that holds a valid Skilled Worker sponsor licence. We identify employers on the Home Office register who are actively hiring in your field, tailor your resume and cover letter for each role, and apply to 30 to 50 positions per month on your behalf. Every application is tracked and reported to you weekly. You focus on preparing for interviews while we handle the pipeline.

Service 04
Teaching in the United Kingdom. Qualified Teacher Status, International Qualified Teacher Status and Assessment Only

Qualified Teacher Status is required to teach in state-maintained schools in England. Whether you are an experienced teacher from overseas seeking Qualified Teacher Status recognition, a working teacher wanting to qualify through Assessment Only, or someone looking to train internationally through the International Qualified Teacher Status programme, we assess which route fits your background and guide you through every requirement. The Assessment Only route, the International Qualified Teacher Status programme, and the Postgraduate Certificate in Education each suit different circumstances, we tell you which applies to yours.
